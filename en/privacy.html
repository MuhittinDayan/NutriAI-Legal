NutriAI Privacy Policy

This policy explains which data may be processed when you use the NutriAI app, what it is used for, and your rights over your data.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, NutriAI may process the following data:

  • Account and profile information
  • Nutrition and training preferences: diet type, allergies, foods you dislike, meal preparation time, training experience and session length (all optional and editable in your profile)
  • Meal logs, water tracking, exercise and weight tracking data
  • Health and activity data such as step counts, only if you allow it
  • Notification preferences and technical information about your device
  • Prompts, text and, if you choose, meal photos that you send to AI features
  • Limited verification data about your subscription and purchases

2. Why we use data

  • To provide a personalised nutrition and health experience
  • To create AI-assisted suggestions, recipes, analyses and plan updates
  • To run meal photo analysis only when you start it
  • To schedule reminders and improve the in-app experience
  • To verify your subscription status and manage premium access
  • To keep basic technical logs for debugging, security and service quality

3. Third-party services

NutriAI may use third-party services to provide the service:

  • Supabase: authentication, database and storage
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement verification
  • Google Gemini: creating analyses and suggestions from the meal photo or text you choose and, depending on the feature, your age, height, weight, goal, nutrition preferences, allergies, disliked foods, training preferences and related activity data. Meal photos are resized before they are sent.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Google Translate: food search text and translation of food names; requests are sent through Supabase

The app asks for your explicit consent before data is first shared with Google Gemini. You can withdraw this consent in your profile. These services are used only as far as needed to run the related feature. Third-party providers may be subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Data retention and security

Data may be kept for as long as needed to provide the service or as required by legal obligations. NutriAI aims to apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data; however, no system is completely free of risk.

5. Deleting data and your rights

You can update your profile information and in-app preferences in the app. In Profile → Delete my account you can permanently delete your account, your profile and the related app records. Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription automatically; manage your subscription in your Apple account settings. Store transaction records are subject to the retention policy of the relevant provider. Contact us if you need help.
